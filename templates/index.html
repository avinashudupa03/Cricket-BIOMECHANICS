Key Capabilities

End-to-end biomechanics analysis from a single batting video.

Pose Estimation

MediaPipe Pose tracks 33 key body landmarks on the batsman across every frame, isolating the correct player throughout the shot.

Joint Angle Analysis

Elbow, knee, shoulder and hip angles computed per frame, producing full angle time-series and precise impact-angle measurements.

Batting Phase Detection

Automatic phase segmentation — Stance, Backlift, Downswing, Impact, Follow-through — with frame-level boundaries.

Shot Classification

Supervised ML models classify cut, defence, drive and flick shots with Leave-One-Out Cross-Validation.

ML Insights

Model comparison, feature importance, and interpretability — full ML evaluation pipeline on the research dataset.

Annotated Video Output

Generated analysis video with tracked skeleton overlay, live batting phase, frame counter and movement score HUD.
